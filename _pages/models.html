Selected modeling work developed across research and engineering projects. These models support flood-risk analysis, hydraulic design, river-corridor assessment, and water resources management.

Hydrodynamics

Probabilistic dam-break flood modeling

Monte Carlo-based workflow for dam-break flood mapping using a 2D local inertial model, designed to quantify uncertainty in flood extent and hazard metrics.

Model comparison

Full momentum vs. local inertial hydrodynamic models

Comparative framework for evaluating trade-offs between full momentum and local inertial approaches under dam-break scenarios.

River corridors

Hydraulic and thermal habitat suitability analysis

Modeling work connecting hydraulic and thermal controls to habitat suitability, persistence, and connectivity in regulated river corridors.

Water infrastructure

Pressurized water distribution and transient analysis

Hydraulic models for transmission mains, pumping stations, reservoirs, and pressurized networks, including water-hammer analysis and surge-protection design.

Water resources

Managed aquifer recharge and green infrastructure assessment

Modeling and analysis developed through BRAMAR, DIGIRES, and SMART-Control-related work on managed aquifer recharge and digital green infrastructure.
