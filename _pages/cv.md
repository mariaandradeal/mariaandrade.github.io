Research experience
======

### Current Ph.D. research

I study hydrological processes and groundwater-surface water interactions to support assessments of remediation effectiveness and habitat protection in the Colorado River corridor under current and future climate conditions.

### Research projects

- **BRAMAR - Strategies and Technologies for Mitigating Water Scarcity in Northeast Brazil**  
  International Brazil-Germany cooperation focused on advanced effluent treatment, reuse, managed aquifer recharge, and integrated water-resources management.
- **DIGIRES - Digital-enabled Green Infrastructure for Sustainable Water Resources Management**  
  Development and use of ICT tools and citizen observations to support artificial recharge and nature-inspired water infrastructure.
- **SMART Control - SMART Framework for Real-Time Monitoring and Control of Subsurface Processes in Managed Aquifer Recharge Applications**  
  Real-time monitoring and control system for aquifer recharge applications, including risk assessment and forecasting tools.
